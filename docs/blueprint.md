# **App Name**: AutoHouse Manager

## Core Features:

- Car Inventory Management: Add, update, and remove car listings with details such as make, model, year, mileage, and price.
- Customer Record Management: Store and manage customer information, including names, contact details, purchase history, and preferences.
- Appointment Booking: Allow customers to book appointments to view cars, with an interface to manage schedules.
- Test Drive Scheduling: Enable customers to schedule test drives and allow staff to manage the test drive calendar.
- Car Sales Management: Facilitate car sales transactions by recording sale details, customer information, and generating sales reports.
- Car Rental Service: Offer car rentals, manage rental agreements, track rental periods, and calculate rental fees.
- Dynamic Car Recommendation: Employ generative AI tool to recommend suitable vehicles to users based on stated needs.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep blue (#1A237E) to evoke trust and sophistication, as cars are major purchases.
- Background color: Light gray (#F5F5F5), nearly white, for a clean and professional look.
- Accent color: Soft lavender (#C5CAE9), a calm analogous hue to the primary color, used sparingly for highlights and active elements.
- Font pairing: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for headlines, and 'Inter' (sans-serif) for body text.
- Use modern, flat icons to represent different car models and functionalities.
- Clean, grid-based layout for easy navigation and clear presentation of car details and schedules.
- Subtle transitions and animations for a smooth user experience when browsing cars or managing appointments.
